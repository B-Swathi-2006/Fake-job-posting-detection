ABOUT THE PROJECT

Responsible job-risk screening

JobShield is a college mini-project demonstrating NLP, supervised learning, explainable rule-based signals, and safe workflow design.

Model

Random Forest is the persisted classifier. BERT can be added as a separate, optional experiment without inventing results.

Limitations

Language patterns are not proof. Website extraction may fail, websites may restrict access, and model quality depends on the real dataset and evaluation split.
